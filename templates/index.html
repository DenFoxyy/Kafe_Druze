Our story

Welcome to Kafe' Druze, the first eastern European cafe in Oslo, where tradition, warmth, and rich flavors come together to create a truly authentic experience where you can enjoy delicious food and drinks in a warm and inviting atmosphere.

Our kitchen is dedicated to preserving authentic recipes passed down through generations. Every dish is prepared with carefully selected ingredients, traditional techniques, and genuine passion.
